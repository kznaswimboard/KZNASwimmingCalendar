How does the conversion work?

A 25m pool has more turns than a 50m pool, and each turn gives the swimmer a small advantage through the push-off.

The formula is simple: count the extra turns in the 25m swim and subtract the estimated time gained from those turns. This gives an equivalent 50m-pool time, making the two swims easier to compare fairly.
